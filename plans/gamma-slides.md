# The Problem

## Bengali misinformation has no defence

- **65% of Bangladeshi Facebook users** have shared a post they later learned was false
- Bengali rumours spread across family group chats in **hours**, not days
- **No major AI tool speaks Bengali well enough** to stop it

---

# The Solution

## TrustLens — paste anything, get a verdict

- Paste a **URL, Bengali text, or image** → trust score in under 2 seconds
- **0 to 100 score** broken across 6 explainable signals
- **Bilingual.** Bengali-native. Web-cited. Fast.

---

# How It Works

## Three-stage AI cascade

- **Stage 1 — Perplexity** for live web search and cross-referencing
- **Stage 2 — Mistral** for Bengali language and sentiment analysis
- **Stage 3 — OpenAI** for the final explainable verdict
- **Graph-RAG on Neo4j** tracks author reputation across history
- **Redis-cached embeddings** return repeat checks in under a second
